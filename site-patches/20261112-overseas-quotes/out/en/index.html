Overseas company registration

The first step in going overseas is to establish a company. This page publishes user quotes for overseas company registration and common related services. Bank accounts and certain other items are available on inquiry.

出海ABC provides a coordination service for overseas company registration. The quote and requirements are reviewed first; a licensed fulfilling supplier then accepts and completes the filing. User quotes are published on this page for comparison and verification.

Fees should be paid directly to that supplier. Passports and identity documents must be submitted through the supplier’s official process. This site does not collect payment, and does not retain identity documents on the page.

Small and medium-sized enterprises, sole proprietors, and source manufacturers generally need to establish a company before entering into contracts, receiving payment, completing customs formalities, or opening a place of business. The figures below are user quotes, compiled on 12 November 2026. Amounts are in renminbi unless a row is marked in US dollars or Singapore dollars. Official fees charged in US dollars, Hong Kong dollars, or Korean won remain in that currency and are not converted on this page.

Quotes on this page may differ from the price at the time of filing because of delayed updates or changes in cost. The latest written quote is authoritative.

Bank accounts, items that are suspended or subject to separate agreement, and special entities whose names include words such as foundation, association, or alliance, are available on inquiry. This page does not estimate a price for those items. Bank names are not listed; available arrangements are confirmed at the time of inquiry.

Questions#

Q: Who holds the licences and qualifications?

A: The fulfilling supplier holds the licences required for the filing and accepts and completes the work. 出海ABC provides coordination, beginning with a review of the quote and requirements.

Q: To whom should fees be paid?

A: Fees should be paid directly to the fulfilling supplier. Passports and identity documents must be submitted through the supplier’s official process. This site does not collect payment or retain identity documents on the page.

Q: Why do some items have no published quote?

A: Bank accounts, items that are suspended or subject to separate agreement, and special entities not listed here may be requested by email or WeChat. This page does not estimate a price for those items.

Q: Is travel required?

A: Inquiries and the submission of documents are completed by email or WeChat. Attendance in person is not required solely for registration. Some jurisdictions have a separate identity verification requirement, such as the UK registrar’s online verification, which will be explained when the plan is confirmed.

Q: Can a filing proceed without an SSN?

A: An inquiry may be made without an SSN. If an SSN or ITIN is already held, it should be included when documents are prepared. User quotes for US state registrations include an EIN application by default.

Q: Which state should be selected?

A: This page lists user quotes by state. The selection is confirmed according to the intended use when the plan is settled; this page does not make that choice on the client’s behalf. A New York LLC is generally not a first choice because of the publication requirement; an INC or another state may be discussed instead.

Q: In which currency are the quotes stated?

A: Amounts are in renminbi unless a row is marked in US dollars or Singapore dollars. Official fees charged in US dollars, Hong Kong dollars, or Korean won remain in that currency and are confirmed in writing.
