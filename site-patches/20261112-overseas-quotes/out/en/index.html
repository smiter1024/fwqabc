Other states and places#

Other US states can also be asked about, including Texas, South Dakota, Maryland, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Pennsylvania, Connecticut, Minnesota, New Mexico, and states not listed in the table. A Texas name usually cannot be searched in advance and has to be filed directly. A Georgia or Pennsylvania INC usually requires publication; ask for that cost.

Company formation, and common annual review or address services, can also be asked about for the United Kingdom, France, Romania, Canada (including Ontario, British Columbia, and a federal company), Hong Kong, Singapore, Korea, Malaysia, and Macau. A name that includes words such as foundation, association, or alliance is usually possible in only some states or places, and has to be checked.

Documents to prepare#

The website does not accept files. These are commonly needed for a US company filing, so they can be gathered early. Documents for other places are confirmed at the time of engagement.

  • A proposed English company name (the supplier can also help with alternatives)
  • A photo of a valid ID, such as a passport, national ID card, or driver licence
  • The address on that ID
  • A phone number and an email address
  • An SSN or ITIN, if you already have one
