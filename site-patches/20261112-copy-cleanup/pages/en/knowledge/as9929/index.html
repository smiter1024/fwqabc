AS9929: whether it fits

A decision note taken from this site's 9929 page. The full text stays there.
1 min
分享到 X

先说结论

The line page says 9929 means China Unicom AS9929, registered as CHINA UNICOM Industrial Internet Backbone. Plans labeled 9929 are usually described as friendlier for Unicom visitors at the evening peak.

适合谁

Sites whose visitors are mainly on China Unicom. The page also says that if you want all three carriers to be less uneven, you can read it next to CN2 GIA and CMIN2.

不要直接照做

Friendlier for Unicom is not a claim that the same plan is friendlier for every carrier. The page has no test result for the other carriers.

怎么确认

The page says real behavior depends on the data center and the region, and this site gives no speed conclusion before its own test. Check that the merchant names 9929, and the public AS9929 record.
